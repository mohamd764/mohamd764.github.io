Questions

Which Trainerize plan do I need?

Studio or Enterprise. Trainerize only offers API access on those plans. We check this on the first call, before you pay anything.

Does it change anything inside Trainerize?

No. HealthSync reads workout and habit completion from Trainerize and weigh-ins from Renpho. It doesn't edit your clients' programmes. The reports and the sheet are separate files you control.

Is the Renpho connection official?

No. Renpho has no public API, so HealthSync uses community-maintained access, which can change without notice. On the Managed plans, keeping it working is Mohamed's job. We'll talk it through openly on the demo call.

Will it show body fat?

Weight is the core of every report. Body composition is shown where the scale reading includes it, and we confirm what your Renpho account actually returns during setup.

Where does my clients' data go?
  • Your Trainerize and Renpho keys stay in a private settings file on the machine that runs the update: your server on Setup only, or a private server Mohamed manages on Managed. They are never put in code, shared documents or email.
  • The Google Sheet lives in your Google account and is shared only with named accounts. No "anyone with the link" sharing.
  • Client pages are marked so search engines don't index them, and they leave out any medication column from your coach sheet.
  • Your client data is never used in marketing. The screenshots on this page are demo data.
  • An NDA is available on request.
  • HealthSync is not HIPAA-certified. If you run a medical or clinical weight-loss practice, let's talk about your requirements before setup.
What happens if I cancel?

Give 30 days' notice. The sheet and the reports are already yours and stay in your account.

Can I get a refund?

If the Trainerize API turns out not to be available on your plan before the build starts, you get the setup fee back in full. Once the build has started, the setup fee isn't refundable.

Kilograms or pounds?

Either. You choose once in the settings.

Can I see it first?

Yes. Book a 20-minute demo and Mohamed will walk you through the reports on demo data, in your colours if you send your logo.
